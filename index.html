About

Design is not decoration — it is clarity.

I'm Adeleke Israel, a graphic designer with eight years of experience helping brands find their visual voice. I work at the intersection of craft and strategy — turning complex ideas into elegant, purposeful design.

My practice spans brand identity, editorial systems, packaging, and art direction. Each project begins with deep listening and ends with something that feels inevitable — like it could not have been any other way.
